## 🎯 The Challenge & Theme

> **Theme:** `#FinanceForAll` — Empowering Filipino Entrepreneurs (*Negosyantes*)  
> **Core Question:** *"How can GCash become the complete financial backbone that empowers Filipino entrepreneurs to run smarter, grow faster, and build a business that lasts?"*

### Challenge Directives:
1. **Identify Real Pain Points:** Uncover operational, logistical, and financial bottlenecks facing Filipino micro, small, and emerging entrepreneurs.
2. **Develop Holistic Ecosystem Solutions:** Build strategies that cross-leverage GCash products (Payments/QR, GLoan, GCredit, GInsure, GSave/GFunds, merchant analytics, B2B supplier rails) to transition *negosyantes* from merely *surviving* to *thriving and scaling*.

---

## 👥 Eligibility & Team Requirements

* **Team Size:** Exactly **5 undergraduate members**.
* **Year Level:** Currently in **3rd, 4th, or 5th year** of undergraduate studies.
* **Inter-University:** Allowed (members can be from different schools/colleges as long as all meet the 3rd–5th year requirement).
* **Technical Requirement:** **None required.** Initial round focuses on business strategy and pitch. In the Hackathon Proper, GCash provides an internal visual / *"vibe"* coding platform with guided onboarding.
* **Fee:** 100% Free / No registration fee. No school endorsement/recommendation letter required.
* **Agreement:** Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) required upon registration.
